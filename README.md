## 🧠 About Me

I'm a Computer Scientist, researcher, and software developer passionate about Artificial Intelligence, optimization algorithms, and intelligent systems.

My work focuses on combining mathematical optimization, computational intelligence, and deep learning to solve complex problems, particularly in Natural Language Processing.

🏛️ **Professor & Researcher** at the [University of Informatics Sciences (UCI)](https://www.uci.cu/universidad/claustro/orlando-grabiel-toledano-lopez).

🔬 **Research interests**
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Deep Learning & Transformer Architectures
- Metaheuristic Optimization
- Neural Network Training Optimization
- Algorithms & Data Structures
- High-Performance Computing

🚀 **Current research:** Metaheuristic optimization approaches for deep neural network training in NLP tasks, including automatic question generation in Spanish.
